About Me

Passionate technologist with expertise in network architectures and cloud systems

I'm currently working as Director of Software Engineering at Graphiant, where I lead software development initiatives and drive innovation in network technologies.

Previously, I served as Tech Lead at Nuage Networks from Nokia, where I contributed to cutting-edge software-defined networking solutions.

I hold a Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Electrical Engineering from Delft University of Technology (TU Delft) in The Netherlands.

Currently based in the San Francisco Bay Area, California, USA, I'm originally from Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India.

My expertise spans Network Architectures and Services, Software-Defined Networking, and Cloud Management Systems.

When I'm not coding or architecting systems, I enjoy traveling, reading, exploring new gadgets, and spending quality time with my family.

Network-as-a-Service Architecture with SDN and NFV

A Proposed Evolutionary Approach for Service Provider Networks

M.P.V. Manthena
TU Delft - Network Architectures and Services

Research Overview

Comprehensive thesis addressing Internet ossification through evolutionary NaaS implementation using SDN and NFV technologies. Includes proof-of-concept implementation and experimental performance evaluation on physical network testbeds.

Key Contributions:
  • Evolutionary approach to NaaS implementation
  • SDN and NFV integration strategies
  • Proof-of-concept physical testbed
  • Performance evaluation and validation
